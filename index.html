FORCED OSCILLATION

Oscillation of a system under the action of an external force that varies periodically with time.

⚡

External force adds energy

Compensates the energy lost to friction and drag.
∿

Steady-state frequency

Equals the frequency of the external force.
Equation of the Driving Force
Fcb = F₀ x cos(ωt + φ)
Fcb: driving force · F₀: force amplitude · ω: angular frequency · φ: initial phase
🧺

Washing machine

A rotating part creates a periodic force that shakes the drum.
🚙

Car suspension

The road surface acts as a defined source of excitation.
🔊

Speaker cone

An electrical signal creates a varying force on the cone.
